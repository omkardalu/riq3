HOW IT WORKS

From Resume Uploads
to Better Screening

Resume IQ brings the entire screening process into one place — from setting job requirements to reviewing candidate results.

01

Start With What You Need

Tell Resume IQ what you are looking for. Add the skills and requirements that matter for the role, and they become the reference for the entire screening process.

02

Bring Your Resumes Together

No need to open and check resumes one at a time. Upload your entire collection of resumes in one go. There is no fixed file-count limit — the total upload can be up to 10 GB.

03

Let RIQ Do the Checking

Once the resumes are uploaded, Resume IQ goes through them and checks the information against your requirements. Skills, experience, education, projects, and relevant keywords are taken into account.

04

See the Score at a Glance

Every resume gets an ATS score that shows how closely it matches the requirements. You can also see which skills are present and which ones are missing.

05

Compare Everyone in One View

Instead of switching between different resumes, see all applicants together. Compare their scores and match percentages and get a quick view of the screening results.

06

Open Any Applicant

Want to know more about someone? Select their profile to see the details found in their resume — including education, experience, projects, strengths, missing skills, weaknesses, and suggestions.

07

Make the Next Step Easier

With the important information already organized, you can spend less time sorting through resumes and more time focusing on the candidates who match your requirements.
